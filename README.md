### About me

I'm a computer-science PhD researcher. My work is about getting structured, usable knowledge out of text and images, using LLMs, RAG, knowledge graphs, and computer vision. Most recently I was **Founding AI Lead at [Nixi AI](https://www.nixiai.ai/de)**, an EXIST-funded medical-AI startup, where I built the AI engineering stack: clinical RAG, German medical ASR, ICD-10-GM coding, and agentic workflows.

- 🔭 I work on **LLMs & RAG**, **knowledge-graph / ontology construction**, **computer vision**, and **clinical AI**
- 🌱 Currently exploring **multi-modal models** and **agentic systems**
- 💞️ Open to collaborating on **open-source GenAI**, **knowledge-graph research**, and **reproducible ML**
- 📫 Reach me at **vamsik.kommineni@outlook.com** or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vkk99)
- ⚡ 7+ years in ML / DL / GenAI; peer-reviewed in *PeerJ Computer Science*

### 🚀 Selected projects

| Project | Description | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **[Inagecas](https://github.com/Vamsi-Kommineni/Inagecas)** | AI customer-support tool that answers tickets from a company's own docs, with guardrails so every answer is grounded in the source (no hallucinations). Handles customer screenshots and passes anything it can't confidently answer to a human. | RAG · Guardrails · ling-3.0-flash-vl · Vision-Language · LLM routing · OpenTelemetry |
| **[Automatic KG & ontology construction](https://github.com/fusion-jena/automatic-KG-creation-with-LLM)** | Pipeline that builds ontologies and knowledge graphs from text with LLMs, with a focus on reproducibility. · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08345) | Knowledge Graphs · Ontology · LLMs |
| **[Multi-LLM information retrieval with RAG](https://github.com/fusion-jena/information-retrieval-using-multiple-LLM-and-RAG)** | Ensembles multiple LLMs with RAG to extract deep-learning methodologies from biodiversity literature. · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.3204) | RAG · LLM ensemble · Information retrieval |
| **[Agentic competency-question generation](https://github.com/fusion-jena/Agentic-CQ-Generation)** | Agentic, retrieval-augmented pipeline that generates, validates, and consolidates competency questions to support KG / ontology development. | AI agents · RAG · Knowledge Graphs |
